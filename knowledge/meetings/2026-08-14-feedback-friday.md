## The discussion, as far as it matters to us

- **"AI ROI is still negative."** Marc cites HBR and MIT. *"I have yet to see one company show what their return on investment is. Cisco couldn't show it."* **Unsourced as quoted.**
- **Circular AI financing.** Marc argued that vendor money circulates among the same handful of companies ("Enron-esque"). It's Marc's opinion, and useful only as background to the ROI skepticism buyers bring into the room.
- **"We sometimes confuse speed with intelligence"** (Daniel). The input data decides output quality, and poisoned or obfuscated data corrupts it.
- **AI doesn't have SaaS scaling economics** (Daniel). There are hard, recurring costs in power and in chips refreshed about every two years, and today's prices are subsidized. It connects directly to the FinOps offering.
- **Role change, not role elimination.** From a conversation Xander had with a retail analytics team: analysts will spend less time building dashboards and more with stakeholders on better metrics. Lorin: undocumented, informal work (the "water cooler" layer) is the risk when a bot replaces a person. *"Let's hope it works the way it's supposed to work."*
- **Model-routing waste** (Marc): people use frontier models as a search engine. Routers (RouteLLM, LiteLLM) can switch models based on context. This belongs in the FinOps story.

**For the vault:** nothing here changes a decision. The value is the ritual itself, and the ROI and cost narratives buyers are hearing.
