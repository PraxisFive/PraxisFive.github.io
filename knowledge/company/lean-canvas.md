# Lean Canvas — PraxisFive

Ash Maurya's Lean Canvas, filled from the vault as at 2026-08-05. The canvas is a **snapshot of current belief**, not a plan — several blocks rest on assumptions that haven't been tested. Those are marked and mapped at the bottom.

---

## 1 · Problem

**The top three:**

1. **They have a working business and no operating model built for the AI era.** They've adopted AI tools. They haven't rewired how the business runs. The tools sit on top of an unchanged operating model and the return never materializes.
2. **A board or sponsor mandate has landed with no credible plan behind it.** Leadership needs a defensible baseline and a sequenced answer fast — and has no independent read on where they actually are.
3. **Nobody serves this well at mid-market scale.** Tier-1 delivery economics don't justify themselves for a $25M–$300M company, and the AI implementation shops solve tooling, not operating model.

**Existing alternatives:**

| Alternative | Why it fails them |
|---|---|
| Tier-1 / MBB strategy firms | Too expensive, too slow, deck-shaped. Priced for enterprise, not mid-market. |
| Boutique AI consultancies | Tool implementation and pilots. Not operating-model change. |
| Internal transformation team | Cannot produce an *independent* read on itself. Also the thing being assessed. |
| Systems integrators | Build what they're told to build. No point of view on what should change. |
| Do nothing / pilot purgatory | The default. Costs nothing visible, which is exactly why it persists. |

---

## 2 · Customer Segments

**Mid-market companies facing the AI transition** — roughly **$25M–$300M** revenue, concentrated in six focus industries: Enterprise B2B Software · AI · Services · FMCG · Packaging · Materials.

Two avenues: **PE and growth equity** (primary, established) and **family offices / family-owned businesses** (forming).

**Early adopters — who we can actually land first:**

- Sponsor-backed companies with a **board-level AI mandate and no plan**
- **Mega flow owners** (CRO, CPO, COO) who own a visibly broken flow and need external evidence to fund the fix
- **PE operating partners** building a post-acquisition value creation plan — one lands, the portfolio follows
- **Warm-intro-reachable accounts.** Trust is the binding constraint at this stage, not addressable market.

---

## 3 · Unique Value Proposition

> **We rewire your operating model for the AI era — and stand behind a 5x minimum return.**
>
> Assessed across all five mega flows. Deep on the one where the value actually is. Value visible in days, not quarters.

**High-level concept:** *MBB rigor at AI-native speed and economics.* Outcomes realized, not reports filed.

**The line that does the work:** *Stuff, not fluff.*

---

## 4 · Solution

1. **The AI Integrated Operating Model** — five mega flows. Assess all five, go deep on one. One model behind every engagement rather than a bespoke framework each time.
2. **Value Assessment → Roadmap → Execution Tracks** — one integrated tool chain, not three documents. Each stage feeds the next automatically.
3. **Five execution tracks** — sprints of up to four weeks for the first three, ~3 months for Strategic Plays, 6 months+ for Transformational Bets. Quick Strikes land value inside the first weeks.
4. **The Continuous AI Realization Service** — the agents, artifacts, and running AI services we leave behind, plus the measurement that proves the 5x actually happened.

## 6 · Revenue Streams

**Engagements** — XS free · S $75K · M ~$150K · L $250–350K · XL custom. Priced on effort (blended ~$350/hour), packaged as sizes.

**Continuous AI Realization Service** — Essential ~$10K/mo · Plus $20K/mo · PraxisFive Advantage $25K/mo. Sold as 1-year and 3-year subscriptions, billed monthly.

**The intended motion:** land the project, convert to Advantage. Project revenue is the landing motion; recurring revenue is the model — and the firm's valuation multiplier.

---

## 7 · Cost Structure

- **Client Pod** — Engagement Manager (~$80K), Technical Analyst, Strategist/Domain Expert, plus admin support
- **Expert Pool and PX5 Talent Network** — 1099, offshore-weighted (South America, Eastern Europe, Canada ~30% below US)
- **Target: delivery cost at or under 50% of project price** — a $75K engagement costs ≤$35K
- **Asset library build** — front-loaded, amortized across future engagements. The reason early engagements may break even.
- **AI tooling and infrastructure**
- **GTM and business development**
- **Target: 30% firm EBITDA**

## 8 · Key Metrics

**The North Star — client value realized against the 5x floor.** Everything else is a leading indicator of it.

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Realized client ROI | ≥5x |
| Recurring revenue as % of total | Rising; Advantage is the target landing |
| Book of business per pod | $1.5–2.5M |
| Project gross margin | ~50% |
| Blended realized rate | ~$350/hour |
| **Asset reuse rate** — % of a delivery drawn from the library | Rising. The margin depends on it. |
| Time to first value | Days — first Quick Strike inside week one |
| Baseline-to-proposal cycle | One meeting to proposal, or one more discovery |

---

## 9 · Unfair Advantage

*The test for this block: what can't be bought or copied?*

- **The models, frameworks, and tools combined with real domain expertise and data access.** Not the models alone — the combination. That is the thing that can't be prompted.
- **IP retained across every engagement.** Each client compounds the asset rather than consuming it. Competitors starting now start from zero.
- **Decades of alliance and ecosystem operating depth** — the ability to walk in with a hypothesis and a point of view already formed, before the data arrives.
- **AI-native delivery economics.** Incumbents can't match them without cannibalizing the leverage pyramid their own P&L depends on.
- **A learning company.** We get better as the models get better, and better again as we learn — compounding that a project-shaped competitor doesn't accrue.

## The riskiest assumptions

The canvas is only as good as what's underneath it. In rough order of what would hurt most if wrong:

| # | Assumption | Block | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mid-market will pay $150K+ for operating-model work | Revenue · Segments | **Untested.** Average engagement value not yet modeled. |
| 2 | Project clients convert to $25K/mo recurring | Revenue | **Untested.** The entire multiplier rests on it. |
| 3 | Delivery can actually hit ~50% margin at these prices | Cost | **Untested.** Depends on asset reuse that doesn't exist yet. |
| 4 | The five mega flows are the right decomposition | Solution | Believed, unvalidated externally. Assessment dimensions not built. |
| 5 | The $25M–$300M band holds outside B2B software | Segments | Calibrated on SaaS. FMCG/Packaging/Materials differ. |
| 6 | Warm intros scale beyond the founder's network | Channels | **The binding constraint.** No second channel proven. |
| 7 | 5x is provable, not just claimable | UVP · Metrics | Requires the Continuous Service to measure it. |
| 8 | Clients accept our IP retention terms | Unfair advantage | Untested at contract on a PraxisFive deal. |
