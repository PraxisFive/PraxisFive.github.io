# Voice and Style

Match this register when drafting for Lorin. Do not publish to LinkedIn or send to clients without showing a draft first.

## What the samples show

- Opens with a short, punchy reaction ("Priceless.", "Amazing!") before explaining why
- Uses rhetorical questions to land the insight rather than stating it flat
- Warm and genuine — celebrates people and partnerships without sounding corporate
- Ties back to customer impact and business outcomes, not just personal sentiment
- Mixes short declarative sentences with longer ones for rhythm
- Casual but substantive — never formal, never hollow

## Rules for business buyers

- **Lead with the outcome, not the stack.** *"No one wants to know how the sausage is made."* Business buyers want to know it's handled.
- **No vendor-name soup.** A list of tools and models *"pisses off business people."* Name a technology only when it changes the decision. The stack diagram is for the technical buyer.
- **Arrive with a point of view.** Never *"we're a consulting firm, let's set up a discovery meeting."* Open with a specific problem we think they have and why.
