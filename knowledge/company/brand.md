## Praxis — the Greek root

**πρᾶξις** · *praxis* · action · practice · execution

- **Literal meaning:** the application of knowledge into the real world.
- **Contrast with *theoria*:** not theory alone — ideas and wisdom transformed into meaningful action.
- **Aristotle's usage:** the highest form of applied wisdom — knowing what to do, and doing it well.

**Praxis is not simply "doing."** It carries five things: thoughtful action · intelligent execution · continuous learning · refinement through experience · meaningful results.

Still used today in medicine, education, engineering, and transformation — wherever knowledge has to become measurable impact.

### Praxis in the AI era

**Knowledge is no longer the scarce resource.** AI creates knowledge; praxis creates outcomes.

> Organizations don't fail because they lack ideas. They fail because execution, adoption, and measurement break down.

**Knowledge alone no longer creates advantage. Execution does.**

### 2 · The five kinds of value — **new**

Every engagement should create all five, and **each builds on the one before it**:

**Better Decisions** → **Better Execution** → **Better Adoption** → **Better Performance** → **Better Outcomes**

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Better Decisions | Sharper choices, made faster, with evidence |
| Better Execution | Plans that convert into delivered work |
| Better Adoption | People actually using what we build |
| Better Performance | Measurable gains in how the business runs |
| Better Outcomes | Value the organization can see and sustain |

Decisions enable execution; execution enables adoption. This is the clearest articulation the firm has of *what an engagement is for*.

### 3 · The organizational AI journey — **canonical**

**Confirmed 2026-08-07.** This replaces the earlier Initiate → Accelerate → Optimize → Scale → Transform set, which is retired. Five levels measuring how far a client has travelled:

| # | Level | State |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | **Emerging** | Experiments and pilots. AI is isolated, value is anecdotal. |
| 2 | **Developing** | Repeatable use cases. Foundations forming, value is local. |
| 3 | **Proficient** | AI embedded in core workflows. Value is measured and real. |
| 4 | **Differentiated** | AI reshapes how the business competes. Value compounds. |
| 5 | **Exponential** | AI redefines the operating model. Value scales non-linearly. |

**Each level isn't a step — it's a magnitude.** Levels 4 and 5 are where value scales non-linearly.

Offering size is now driven off this scale — a Level-1 Emerging client at a large enterprise may correctly be an S; a Level-5 Exponential client at a mid-market company may correctly be an XL. **Most organizations sit at different levels in different domains**, so the read is per-flow rather than a single score. Carried into [Engagement architecture](engagement-architecture.md) and [Service offerings](service-offerings.md).

### How we engage against the journey

The levels are **not a scorecard — they are a starting point.**

- **ASSESS** — where are you today? Maturity diagnosed across all five domains, not just technology. Most organizations sit at different levels in different areas.
- **PARTNER** — no handoff at deployment. We stay through adoption, measurement, and the next level up.
- **ELEVATE** — one level at a time. Level 5 isn't the entry point; durable gains come from advancing deliberately, not skipping stages.

> **Why five is in the name:** five domains to assess, five levels to climb, five kinds of value to prove. It isn't symbolism — it's the shared operating language between us and every client.

### Cultural resonance

Five senses · five fingers · five-point star · five elements · five stages. Five is large enough to be complete, small enough to be memorable.

## Visual identity — settled 2026-08-13

The brand guide landed. Colour is decided; the earlier "navy and blue are arbitrary" position and the 10-option plum exploration board are both superseded.

### Primary palette

| Role | Colour | Hex | Use |
|------|--------|-----|-----|
| **Signature** | Praxis Plum | `#793268` | Key elements, highlights, brand presence |
| **Energy / technology** | Electric Blue | `#4267FF` | CTAs, links, icons, forward motion |
| **Authority** | Deep Titanium | `#24262D` | Primary text, headers, structure |
| **Clarity** | Ice | `#F2F6FA` | Backgrounds, clean modern layouts |

### Accent palette

Lilac `#B283F1` · Cyan `#00C2D9` · Teal `#20D0B6` · Violet `#6B4CFF` · Magenta `#E23E8C`

Accents carry data visualisation, emphasis, and "innovation, optimism and human energy." They are not primaries — used sparingly, they keep the plum signature distinctive.

### Neutrals

Ink `#0E1117` · Charcoal `#24262D` · Slate `#4B5263` · Stone `#E6E9EF` · Cloud `#F2F6FA` · White `#FFFFFF`

### Gradients

**Praxis Plum → Electric Blue** and **Praxis Plum → Deep Titanium.** Both appear in the guide's usage examples for covers and hero panels.

### Tints and shades

Praxis Plum runs at 90 / 75 / 50 / 25 / 10 in both directions. **Tints for backgrounds and subtle elements; shades for text, emphasis, and layering.**

> ⚠️ **One thing the guide doesn't solve.** Praxis Plum is dark (L≈0.06), so *small text on Deep Titanium* lands around 1.6:1 — unreadable at kicker sizes. **The fix is a neutral, not a diluted plum.** Small accent text on dark surfaces uses Stone `#E6E9EF`; the decks encode this as a separate `ACCD` token. Diluting plum toward pink to make it legible reads as an off-palette colour and should be avoided — if plum can't be read at that size, use a named neutral instead.
>
> Plum stays full-strength everywhere it can: all fills and bars, large display numerals on dark (a 100pt section numeral reads as a deliberate tonal mark), and all accent text on Ice.

### Logo

Wordmark is **Praxis** in white or Deep Titanium + **Five** in Electric Blue, with the descriptor **OUTCOME REALIZATION FIRM** set small, all-caps, letter-spaced beneath. No clear-space or minimum-size rules yet.

### Brand attributes

**Distinctive** (a signature plum that stands apart) · **Modern** (paired with electric blue) · **Trusted** (grounded in deep titanium) · **Forward-thinking** (accents bring innovation and human energy)
