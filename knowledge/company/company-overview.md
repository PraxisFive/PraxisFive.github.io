# Company Overview

**PraxisFive is an AI-native outcome realization firm.**

We help mid-market companies rewire their operating model for the AI era — across the full lifecycle of the business. New revenue and existing revenue. New customers and existing customers.

Every engagement runs against one model: the **AI Integrated Operating Model** and its five mega flows. We assess all five, then focus where the value is. Every engagement follows one spine: **Baseline → Roadmap → Execution Tracks.** Every engagement is contextually relevant, outcome-based, delivers fast value, and stands behind a **5x minimum ROI**.

We want to be known as the firm that has the stuff, not the fluff.

**Founder:** Lorin Coles — senior operator background in enterprise alliances, ecosystems, and services delivery, including building the joint services delivery model for what was the world's largest Alliance partnership at the time.

**Core team:** Lorin (Executive) · Marc (Engineer) · Jesse (Expert, GTM) · Xander (workflow and data). Backgrounds and roles are in [Team](team.md). Titles and equity aren't settled.

**Entity:** to be an **LLC** (2026-09-09).

**Revenue model:** Engagements sized XS (free) through XL ($350K+), plus the **Continuous AI Realization Service** — recurring advisory and managed service at three levels ($10K–$25K/month). Paid via ACH. Pipeline in HubSpot. Financials in QuickBooks.

**Stage:** Early — establishing the category, building credibility, landing first PraxisFive engagements. **In stealth** as of September 2026. The website is being built but not launched.
