# The AI Integrated Operating Model

The single model that sits behind everything PraxisFive does. Five **mega flows** — the cross-functional flows every business runs, each one a place where AI removes friction and creates enterprise and customer value.

**How we use it.** Every engagement assesses all five. Then one becomes the dominant area of focus, chosen for where the value actually is for that client. The engagement touches all five; it goes deep on one.

- **Ideas → outcomes.** On the left, *"everything that's possible, connecting out to the network"* (the ecosystem). On the right, *"the customer … and the outcomes they're trying to deliver."*

### From mega flow to use case

**Mega flow → flow → use case** (Lorin, [2026-08-14](../meetings/2026-08-14-gtm-mega-flow-working-session.md)). Each mega flow gets:

1. **A framework:** our point of view on the space
2. **About 10 flows:** the problems we see in the market, named at the right altitude
3. **At least one use case per flow:** a specific, proven way AI solves it (up to five)
4. **Packages:** see [Service offerings](service-offerings.md)

The goal is *"a book"* of about 20 proven use cases per mega flow that a client can pick from. It answers field feedback that the mega flows alone read as abstract. Buyers want something tangible. **GTM is the first flow being built out this way** (below).

## 1 — Strategic & Operational Planning

*How the business decides and commits.*

Every business builds a strategy. Every business has to turn that into an annual strategic plan and an operating plan that actually supports the corporate strategy. Every year, it runs annual budgeting and planning — and every function and every area of the business builds its own plan and its own budget underneath that.

This flow is full of friction: disconnected plans, misaligned budgets, accountability that evaporates between the corporate deck and the functional one. It is a tremendous opportunity for AI to enhance the flow, eliminate the friction, and drive genuinely better alignment and accountability.

**Includes joint planning.** Alliances and partnerships generate a large volume of joint business plans and joint go-to-market plans. We intend to be masters and experts at that workflow, with offerings built specifically to support it.

**What we work on:** corporate strategy → operating plan translation · annual planning and budgeting cycles · functional plan alignment · accountability and cadence design · joint business plans and partner GTM plans.

**Lorin wants to lead this flow himself** (2026-08-13). *"Such a manual process … even the best of the best don't do it well."*

## 2 — Innovation & Product Development

*How the business creates what it sells.*

Everything a company does around innovation and new product development, plus the management of existing products and the creation of solutions and offerings.

The opportunity is in bringing cross-functional stakeholders together across the whole arc — from ideation, into development, into deployment of solutions into the market. Quick first wins. Alpha and beta release. First real wins. Then rinse and repeat. Then scale.

**The portfolio lens — Geoffrey Moore's four zones.** We use *Crossing the Chasm* and *Zone to Win* as working models. Every offering sits in one of four zones, and each zone has different metrics and different needs:

- **Incubation zone** — new bets, not yet material
- **Transformation zone** — the one big play being scaled to material revenue
- **Performance zone** — the revenue-producing core
- **Productivity zone** — the efficiency and enablement engine

Companies need to leverage AI differently in each zone. We help them manage the portfolio *across* zones, not optimize one at the expense of the others.

**What we work on:** ideation and innovation process · new product/offering development · existing product and offering management · alpha/beta to first-win motion · portfolio management across the four zones · zone-appropriate metrics.

## 3 — Ecosystem Orchestration & Governance

*How the business positions and partners.*

Critically: **this is not just partner ecosystem.** There are three ecosystems, and we work across all of them.

**Business ecosystem.** The overall market an organization operates within, and all the companies it interacts with — including the companies that influence it without transacting with it.

**Customer ecosystem.** Ours, but far more importantly *our client's customer ecosystem*. Our clients operate inside that ecosystem, and our job is helping them integrate into it and influence within it.

**Partner ecosystem.** Nested inside the customer ecosystem — the strategic partners that make up how a business creates, delivers, and captures value. We help clients optimize those relationships.

Orchestration and governance across all three is the work.

**What we work on:** ecosystem mapping and positioning · influence strategy · strategic partner selection and optimization · governance models and operating cadence · value creation/delivery/capture across partners.

## 4 — GTM Activation & Execution

*How the business wins and realizes value.*

The area PraxisFive is best known for and strongest in. Top of funnel, middle of funnel, bottom of funnel — but we see it as an **hourglass**, and we work it in both orientations: on its side and standing vertically. The hourglass doesn't stop at the close; it widens again into adoption, retention, and expansion.

Everything needed to optimize and drive **velocity** across go-to-market. Velocity is the operative word — acceleration is just speed, but velocity is speed *with direction*. That's what we give clients: friction eliminated, fastest time to value, pointed the right way.

**This is where value realization becomes visible.** Client value realization is our North Star, and it's within this mega flow that we most directly see whether the client actually realized value from our work.

**What we work on:** demand generation and top-of-funnel · pipeline conversion and middle-funnel velocity · close motion and bottom-of-funnel · post-close adoption, retention, and expansion · friction removal across the full hourglass · time-to-value compression · value realization measurement.

### Built out first: the GTM framework (2026-08-14)

**This is the lead mega flow.** It's where Lorin and Jesse are most credible, and *"everybody needs more revenue."* Source: [GTM working session](../meetings/2026-08-14-gtm-mega-flow-working-session.md).

- **Shape.** Lorin drew it as a **bow tie**: wide into the transaction, narrow through it, wide again after. That's the same shape as the hourglass above. **"Bow tie" is Winning by Design's term** (they call it "revenue architecture"), so decide which word we own. PraxisFive goes further past the transaction than they do: *"it doesn't end at the transaction."*
- **Scope.** Top, middle and bottom of funnel, plus everything after the contract (onboarding, customer success, services, partner fulfilment). Push, pull, or both, depending on the market.
- **The buyer is the CRO or Chief Commercial Officer**, because they own every route to market. Not the head of sales or the CMO alone. See [Buyer personas](buyer-personas.md).
- **What good looks like:** revenue that is **repeatable, predictable and durable**. Winning one deal isn't the goal. Being able to do it again, forecast it, and hold the margin is.
- **Cross-functional is the point.** Marketing owns the top of the funnel in one system and sales owns the rest in another, often through modules that arrived by acquisition. Nobody owns the whole bow tie.
- **Alliances baked in, not bolted on.** Partners are part of the motion from day one, not a channel added at the end.
- **The assessment finds the real bottleneck.** *"They may think the problem is here, but the problem was actually here."* It's often the value proposition, or a compensation plan rewarding the wrong behavior.

**The flows (draft).** Named in session. **Lorin owns restating them.**

1. Optimizing against the wrong target
2. Incentivizing the wrong behavior (compensation)
3. No integrated, end-to-end sales and marketing motion
4. Not integrated with product. *Use case: new product feature release (below).*
5. No single source of truth
6. No flywheel: the bottom of the funnel doesn't feed the top
7. Sales and marketing don't understand each other's metrics
8. Dissatisfaction caught too late: churn surfaces after it's happened
9. **Propensity signals for renewal and retention.** Predict churn before it happens (Marc has built this before).
10. *(candidate)* Weak conversion into real qualified pipeline, and deals stalling on "fear of messing up"
- *Bonus, cross-cutting:* **DNA.** A transactional culture can't run an outcome-based motion.

**Use cases in hand:**
- **Feature release → revenue** (Jesse). Field signals show a below-the-line feature (global currency) is blocking deals. It gets reprioritized and built in a week, with pricing, website and collateral updated alongside. Two deals close within four weeks. The workflow then compounds: dev status change → notification → agent-drafted web, blog and LinkedIn updates → a closed-lost review for the feature. *Confirm the figures with Jesse and anonymize before external use.*
- **The closed-loop listening engine** (Jesse). Signals → enrichment → scoring → personalized multi-channel outreach → CRM, run for about the cost of four paid-search clicks a month. "Closed loop" (Lorin's term) means listening that always ends in prioritized action.
- **Client onboarding → churn and NRR** (Lorin), raised as an example of the fishing-with-a-point-of-view approach.

## 5 — Infrastructure Enablement & Readiness

*What the business runs on, in the AI era.*

The systems, the data, the processes, the people — everything required to operate effectively as an AI-era business.

Part of this is the structural shift from a **pyramid model to a diamond model**: fewer layers of pure leverage at the bottom, a thicker middle of capable operators working with AI, a different shape of organization entirely.

**And it is emphatically not just IT.** Infrastructure here means every factor required to support an organization in maximizing its value from AI — technical, operational, procedural, and human.

**What we work on:** systems and platform readiness · data foundations and access · process redesign for AI · workforce capability, roles, and org shape · pyramid-to-diamond transition · governance and change management for AI adoption.

**Where we stop.** We don't resell or configure infrastructure. We lead the transformation, sitting between the client and their VAR ([decision 2026-08-13](../decisions/decision-log.md)). **Strategic IT services are in scope,** and the first one is **AI FinOps / token economics**: making AI spend visible and controllable (see [Service offerings](service-offerings.md)). Lorin: *"IT people need a friend right now."*
